# OUTPOST ZERO — Changelog

## Production Refactor & Native Windows Release

### Architecture & Engine Core
- Decomposed the monolithic `src/game/engine.ts` into modular systems (`src/core/Engine.ts`, `GameLoop.ts`, `GameState.ts`, `EventBus.ts`, `Config.ts`, `InputManager.ts`, `AssetManager.ts`) while keeping `src/game/engine.ts` as a prototype-compatible re-export.
- Replaced per-frame manual timers and `80ms` software timeouts with a single fixed-timestep accumulator `GameLoop` (`1/60s` step).
- Added explicit `MatchStateMachine` lifecycle transitions and duplicate match-end guards.

### Rendering, Sky, Water & 10.24 km² Island Redesign
- Fixed the black sky artifact, terrain mipmap blur (`islandTerrainColor` `repeat=1` instead of `460`), and diagonal storm cylinder sky slice using `SkySystem` (camera-anchored analytical sky shader dome locked to sunlit daylight on the Island map) and a 1,400m vertical-fading electric cyan hexagonal energy lattice `buildStormWallMesh` shader.
- Built 64 `64×64` high-resolution LOD 0 streamed terrain chunks (`IslandTerrainStreamer`) starting at LOD 1 (`32×32`) with synchronous near-player `ensureHighLodAround` LOD 0 upgrade on touchdown, `sampleActiveRenderedHeightAt` active-triangle parity, 5m skirts, northern alpine strata & snow caps (`wy > 54 && wz < -380`), calibrated green hillslopes, and 4-pass smoothed road-grade embankments (`ensureRoadProfiles`).
- Added `src/world/Geology.ts` (`buildIslandGeology`) with 420 multi-tiered stratified granite/limestone cliff outcrops and 240 riverbank/reservoir shoreline boulder clusters anchored deep into terrain slopes while strictly respecting road, POI, building, and reservoir clearances.
- Upgraded `Roads.ts` & `Buildings.ts` with terrain-proud `+0.36m` 4-vertex extruded asphalt embankments (`-3.4m` skirts), galvanized steel steep-drop guardrails, monumental concrete bridges (tilted deck alignment, 2.2m box girders, concrete sidewalks/parapets, `-18m` riverbank abutments, `-24m` bedrock piers), deeply rooted `-8m` concrete foundations + stepped entrances + supported roof ramps on all 16 POIs (including a `-34m` bedrock gravity Hydroelectric Dam + spillways + penstocks and multi-tier glass curtain wall Central City skyscraper), plus 12 highway roadside fortified checkpoints.
- Added `InlandWaterShader` in `Water.ts` matching the ocean slate-navy GLSL water palette with directional downstream current ripples and subtle shoreline bank foam for the elevated Hydro Dam reservoir lake and 7-column river channel, paired with elliptical/channel water surface checks (`getWaterSurfaceYAt`) so reservoir/river landings enter swimming cleanly.
- Fixed oak tree branch seating (`Vegetation.ts`) inside trunk cores, added water-zone and active-triangle divergence checks (`sampleSafeVegY`) eliminating floating grass/trees, implemented an articulated **3D Third-Person Skydiver & Paratrooper Rig** (`buildPlayerSkydiverRig` / `updateSkydiverRigPose` in `src/entities/Parachute.ts`) with third-person orbit camera during freefall and parachuting followed by seamless FPS transition on landing (`Engine.ts`), and added collision/terrain clamping for spectator camera orbit.
- Converted `TacticalHudPanels.tsx` top and bottom HUD bars into collision-free Flexbox 3-column tracks with responsive quick-use stacking below `1024px` and wrapping airborne banners (verified overlap-free at `794×700` and `1280×720`) and positioned `DevPerfOverlay` cleanly below the POI pin badge.
- Built 16 differentiated POIs (including Central City skybridge/HVAC props, Solar Research Array geodesic dome & radio mast, and Coastal Port twin gantry cranes & cargo freighter ship) connected by 24 curved highway/rural/dirt roads with slope-pitched guardrails, bridges, instanced utility poles, sunlit stylized `flatShading` conifers/oaks/boulders, 5,200 instanced grass clusters, and strict footprint exclusion zones.
- Upgraded the C-130J transport aircraft with tactical matte gunmetal + gold stripe livery, tapered wings, 4 spinning props, cockpit glazing, cargo ramp lighting, and scenic SW->NE inland deployment corridor (`DeploymentSystem`).

### Gameplay, HUD, Minimap, Tactical Map & Menus
- Implemented `StormController`, `InventorySystem`, `LootManager`, `DeploymentSystem`, armor plates (`[5]`), medkits (`[6]`), ground loot interaction (`[E]`), full-screen Tactical Inventory & Loadout grid modal (`InventoryModal.tsx` with `[Tab]` toggle open/close & `[Esc]` support), prone stance (`[V]`), obstacle mantle assist, swimming/diving physics, and post-elimination spectator mode.
- Added unified Outpost Zero winged-shield emblem (`BrandEmblem.tsx`) across Title lobby, HUD, Tactical Map, and Inventory headers, visual 4-card weapon silhouette bar, vertical `ALT`/`SPD` airdrop ladder & `[F / SPACE] JUMP` bar, and strictly hid weapon/ammo/crosshair UI during C-130 flight and freefall/parachute descent.
- Updated `RadarMinimap.tsx` and `TacticalMapModal.tsx` to share `MapTransform`, fixed North-Up `N/E/S/W` circular minimap bezel (`minimapRotate: false` default) with standalone `[M] MAP` button, 3-column Tactical Map (`ISLAND OVERVIEW`, `LEGEND`, interactive zoom/pan/waypoint canvas with de-conflicted POI pill offsets, Compass Rose, `MATCH INFO`, `TACTICAL OVERVIEW`, `SCALE`), and eliminated hidden bot wallhack reveals on the island.
- Updated `ModalOverlay.tsx` and `GraphicsSettings.tsx` with Battle Royale-specific copy, placement/damage/survival metrics, quit confirmation, Low/Medium/High/Ultra presets, FOV/ADS sensitivity/Invert Y/Toggle Sprint-Crouch/HUD Scale/Time-of-Day/Accessibility options, and toggleable `[F8]` telemetry overlay (`DevPerfOverlay`).

### Native Windows App (C++20 + C# WinUI 3)
- Added `windows/OutpostZero.Native/` C++20 DLL (`OutpostZero.Native.dll`) exposing DXGI GPU adapter/VRAM enumeration, monitor refresh-rate detection, `ClipCursor` pointer confinement, MMCSS thread priority, and QPC microsecond timing.
- Added `windows/OutpostZero.WinUI/` C# `.NET 10` WinUI 3 host (`OutpostZero.exe`) bundling Vite `dist/` via virtual host mapping, `[F9]` hardware diagnostics bar, `[F11]` borderless fullscreen toggle, and `CoreWebView2` crash recovery UI.
- Updated `package.json` (`windows:build`, `windows:run`, `windows:native`) and `launch-windows.bat` to launch the standalone WinUI 3 app.
